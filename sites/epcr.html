In Silico PCR Tool

Virtual PCR simulation, primer binding analysis, and amplicon prediction.

Read Instructions

Virtual (in silico) or electronic PCR (ePCR) for primers/probes, miRNA or gRNA binding site prediction. Eliminates"off-target" effects against whole genomes, predicts PCR products and searches for potential mismatches.

Alternative: For unlimited genome sizes, use the command-line in silico PCR Java application.

Retrieve sequence from NCBI by accession — chromosomes (NC_), mRNA (NM_), RefSeq contigs
Upload local FASTA file — plain FASTA, multi-FASTA, or sequences
